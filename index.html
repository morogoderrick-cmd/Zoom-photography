Good To Know

QUESTIONS WE GET OFTEN

How early should I book?

For weddings, book as early as you can, ideally 2 to 3 months ahead, because popular dates fill up. Portraits and small shoots can often be arranged within a week.

How long until I get my photos and videos?

Photos are usually ready within 7 to 14 days. Wedding films take a little longer, and we agree the delivery date with you when you book.

Do you travel outside Eldoret?

Yes. We cover events across Kenya. Travel and accommodation outside Eldoret are quoted separately, and we confirm everything before you pay.

How do I pay to secure my date?

A deposit holds your date, and the balance is paid on or before the event day. We accept M-Pesa and bank transfer.

Can I get a custom package?

Absolutely. Tell us what you need in the booking form and we will put together a quote that fits your event and your budget.
